About Me
At A Glance

个人概览

A creative project and marketing professional connecting brand narrative, artist collaboration and real-world visitor experience.

一位将品牌叙事、艺术家合作与真实到场体验连接起来的创意项目及市场管理人。

Core Position

核心定位

Marketing, exhibitions, spatial curation, visual systems and bilingual project communication.

市场营销、展览策划、空间策展、视觉系统与中英双语项目沟通。

I work across the point where creative ideas need to become real environments, real schedules and real visitor touchpoints. My experience covers integrated brand marketing, event planning, art exhibitions, spatial curation and retail-facing campaigns.

我的工作集中在创意概念如何变成真实空间、真实时间表与真实访客体验的过程中。经验涵盖整合品牌营销、活动策划、艺术展览、空间策展及商业零售场景活动。

I am comfortable moving between concept development, client presentation, supplier coordination, production follow-up, visual direction and on-site execution. The goal is always to keep the idea clear while making the delivery practical.

我熟悉从概念发展、客户提案、供应商协调、制作跟进、视觉统筹到现场执行的完整流程。重点是在保持创意清晰的同时，让执行真正可落地。

My design background also lets me independently develop key visuals, identity systems, wayfinding, presentation materials and social media visuals when a project needs speed and consistency.

平面设计背景也让我能够在项目需要效率与统一度时，独立推进主视觉、识别系统、导视、提案物料及社交媒体视觉。
